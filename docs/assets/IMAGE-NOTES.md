# Project imagery

## Transparent product photo

- File: `skyblue-radio-transparent.png`.
- Input: the radio and loop-antenna photograph supplied by the project owner.
- Method: built-in `image_gen`, background-extraction edit, transparent-background output.
- Output: RGBA PNG; transparency includes the circular opening inside the antenna.
- Date: 2026-11-05.

The product photograph was cleaned and its soft details enhanced for presentation. Screenshots and screen animations are generated/captured separately from the firmware; the product photo is not a capture of a running screen.

### Final image-generation prompt

> Use case: background-extraction. Edit target: the attached photo of the sky-blue pocket radio with its circular blue Donut WB Antenna. Create a clean, polished high-resolution product cutout as a PNG with a genuinely transparent alpha background. Preserve the exact radio and connected ring antenna as pictured: the sky-blue rectangular housing, black screen, single right-hand knob, gold connector, and circular dark-blue antenna. Preserve the readable printed text 'Donut WB Antenna' and '10kHz–180MHz' accurately. Remove all white background INCLUDING the empty circular hole inside the antenna, and remove the unrelated thin fragment at the extreme right edge. Keep the antenna and radio fully visible, centered, with modest transparent padding. Improve the soft/low-resolution edges gently and lighting/clarity while preserving shape, proportions, colors and all physical details. Do not invent controls, extra devices, branding or a screen UI; keep the screen dark. No solid background, no checkerboard baked into pixels, no decorative scene or text additions.
