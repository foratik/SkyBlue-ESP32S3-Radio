# Public source history

This public history was reconstructed from the versioned source snapshots.
Captured hardware MAC addresses, raw serial/install/telemetry logs, per-device
measurement records and private backup hashes were excluded from every commit.
Validation JSON files contain non-identifying test summaries only; their checksums
were regenerated. The complete original development history remains local.

Firmware source, compiled applications, bootloader, partition references and
display artwork are preserved. Public commit IDs therefore differ from the local
development archive, while version names and firmware contents remain consistent.
